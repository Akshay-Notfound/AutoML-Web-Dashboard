### 🌟 System Overview

**AutoML Web Dashboard** is a comprehensive automated machine learning and business intelligence workspace. It allows data teams and business stakeholders to upload raw datasets, execute automated exploratory data analysis, train and benchmark multiple ML models, and generate predictive supply chain risk assessments.

---

### 🔬 Operational Architecture & Data Pipeline Flow

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Upload Raw CSV / Excel Dataset] --> B[Automated EDA: Missing Values, Outliers & Distributions]
    B --> C[Automated Encoding, Scaling & Feature Selection]
    C --> D[Multi-Model Tournament: RF, XGBoost, LightGBM, SVM]
    D --> E[Hyperparameter Optimization & Cross-Validation]
    E --> F[Leaderboard Generation: Accuracy, F1, RMSE, ROC-AUC]
    F --> G[Interactive Model Export & Inference Simulator]
    F --> H[Supply Chain Demand & Inventory Risk Analytics]
```

---

### 🚀 Key Capabilities & Features

- **⚙️ One-Click Data Preprocessing:** Automated imputation, standard scaling, and categorical one-hot/label encoding.
- **🏆 Multi-Model Tournament:** Trains and evaluates a fleet of regression and classification models in parallel.
- **📦 Supply Chain Intelligence:** Dedicated forecasting modules for demand variability, stockout risk, and safety stock levels.
- **💾 Model Export & Persistence:** Download trained serialized `.pkl` pipelines ready for production deployment.

